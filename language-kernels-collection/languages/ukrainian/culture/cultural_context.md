# Cultural Context & Genres (Ukrainian)

## 1. Author Archetypes (Extracted from uk_poetry_library.py)
- **SHEVCHENKO**: Kobsar style, biblical allusions, archetypes (grave, steppe), liberty struggle.
- **FRANKO**: Kamenyar spirit, philosophical realism.
- **UKRAINKA**: Neoromanticism, willpower, overcoming imagery.
- **STUS**: Existential tension, palimpsests, hermeticism.
- **ZHADAN**: Urbanism, social drive.

## 2. Cultural Semantic Layers (AAM-V2.1 Protocol)
- **OST6 (Defensive Sarcasm)**: Used for social commentary, amortizes dissonance.
- **OST9 (Sacred Patriotism)**: Combines historical and sacred discourse.
