# Poetic Forms & Styles (Ukrainian)

## 1. Poetic Styles (Source: uk_poetry_library.py)
- **FOLK_8_6**: Kolomyika style (7+7 syllables).
- **CLASSICAL**: Traditional forms (Sonnet).
- **URBAN_VERS_LIBRE**: Modern urban free verse.
- **FABLE**: Narrative verse.

## 2. Formal Structures (Extracted from metadata)
- **Kolomyika Pattern**: 6/8 rhythm, 14 syllables (7+7).
- **Iambic Pentameter**: Associated with philosophical realism (Franko).
- **Dolnik**: Associated with neoclassicism (Ukrainka).
