# Rhythmic & Song Patterns (Ukrainian)

## 1. Patterns
- **Kolomyika Pattern**: Defined in `rhyme_patterns.json` as 14 syllables (7+7) with a 6/8 rhythmic feel. 
- **Rhythmic Complexity**: Ranges from simple folk meters to complex contemporary urban free verse.

## 2. Text-Music Alignment (Conceptual)
- **Constraint**: Syllabic density affects musical phrasing.
- **Source**: Inferred from `uk_rhyme_engine.py` and `geminiService.ts` prompt logic regarding prosody and musicality.

## 3. Validation
These patterns are currently identified as `experimental` or `declarative` within the Ukrainian Poetry Studio code base and have not yet been rigorously tested in a standalone musical alignment context.
