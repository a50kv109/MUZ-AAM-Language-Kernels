# Package Index & Module Architecture: Italian Musical–Literary Kernel (`it-music-poetry`)

## 1. Scope & Domain Boundaries
This module establishes a formal, machine-readable Italian linguistic kernel for music literacy, prosody, poetics, text–music alignment, and traditional vocal performance. It bridges physical acoustics, symbolic notation, metrical versification, and performance pragmatics.

## 2. Architectural Separation & AXIS10 Alignment
All concepts are mapped onto the canonical 10-Axis Architecture (`AXIS10`) of `AAM-Language-Kernels` without redefining any master axis meanings:

* **`AXIS-01: Phonetics & Phonology`** — Prosodic accents, rhyming phonemes, assonance, alliteration (`POE-RHYM-*`).
* **`AXIS-02: Morphology & Word Formation`** — Metrical syllabification, elision, synalepha, dieresis (`POE-SYLL-*`).
* **`AXIS-03: Syntax & Structural Grammar`** — Musical phrasing, poetic meter, verse structure, macro-forms, syncopation syntax (`MUS-RHY-*`, `MUS-MEL-*`, `POE-METR-*`, `POE-FORM-*`, `MUS-FORM-*`).
* **`AXIS-04: Semantics & Conceptual Mapping`** — Acoustic physics ($f, A, I$), tonal mechanics, intervals, scales, instrument register (`MUS-ACOUST-*`, `MUS-PITCH-*`, `MUS-HARM-*`, `MUS-INST-*`).
* **`AXIS-05: Pragmatics & Discourse Analysis`** — Performance pragmatics, dynamics ($p, f$), tempo markings, expressive articulation, text–music interpretative synthesis (`MUS-EXPR-*`, `SYN-ALIGN-*`, `CUL-GEN-*`).
* **`AXIS-06: Etymology & Diachronic Evolution`** — Historical development of genres (e.g., *madrigale*, *bel canto*).
* **`AXIS-07: Sound Symbolism & Phonaesthemics`** — Strictly reserved for acoustic sound symbolism, madrigalisms (word painting where musical sound directly mirrors poetic imagery), and phonaesthetic resonance.
* **`AXIS-08: Orthography & Writing Systems`** — Musical notation symbols, clefs, staff, accidentals, poetic punctuation (`MUS-NOT-*`, notation symbols).
* **`AXIS-09: Sociolinguistics & Dialectology`** — Regional Italian vocal conventions, operatic performance traditions (`CUL-GEN-*`).
* **`AXIS-10: Computational & Cross-Linguistic Alignment`** — Cross-lingual equivalents (EN, DE) and machine-readable ID alignment.
